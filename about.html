Brody.io Documentation

Documentation for Brody.io, a browser-based trading simulation game hosted at https://brody.jagansharma.com.

1. Overview

Brody.io is a trading simulation game with incremental, city-building mechanics. The player manages cash, net worth, stock trading, and upgrades.

The game interface includes:

  • Real-time trading dashboard
  • Portfolio and market overview
  • City-building and upgrade system
  • Auto-save every 30 seconds

2. Game Interface Components

2.1 Header

  • Title: BRODY.IO
  • Tagline: Build Your Empire
  • Navigation buttons: "New Game" and "Continue"
  • Auto-save indicator: "Progress auto-saves every 30 seconds"

2.2 Tutorial / Stepper

  • Step indicator: e.g., "STEP 1 OF 7"
  • Intro text and guidance messages
  • Buttons: "Next", "Skip"

2.3 Dashboard

ComponentDescription
CashCurrent cash available for trading
Net WorthTotal net worth including holdings
Today P&LProfit or Loss for the current day
Passive/minPassive income earned per minute
Day CounterCurrent in-game day

2.4 Market & Trading

  • Select a stock from the market
  • Controls for adjusting buy/sell quantity (+1, +5, +10, etc.)
  • Buttons: "BUY" and "SELL"
  • Displays cost and shares owned

2.5 Portfolio

  • Displays cash, holdings, net worth, and all-time P&L

2.6 City / Buildings

  • Buildings generate passive cash per minute
  • Upgrade system: Up to 3× per building for bonuses
  • Permanent upgrades to improve trading and income

3. Game Mechanics

  • Auto-saving every 30 seconds
  • Incremental upgrades that compound earnings
  • Trading decisions affect cash, net worth, and P&L
  • City buildings produce passive income continuously

4. UI/UX Notes

  • Step-based tutorial guides new players
  • Interactive controls with incremental adjustments for trading
  • Dashboard provides instant feedback on financial stats
  • Visual hierarchy separates trading, portfolio, and city components
