Brody.io Documentation

Developer Guide for brody.jagansharma.com

1. Overview

Brody.io is a trading simulation game with incremental, city-building mechanics. Players manage cash, net worth, stock trading, and upgrades.

  • Real-time trading dashboard
  • Portfolio and market overview
  • City-building and upgrade system
  • Auto-save every 30 seconds

2. Game Interface Components

3. Game Mechanics

  • Auto-saving every 30 seconds
  • Incremental upgrades compound earnings
  • Trading affects cash, net worth, and P&L
  • City buildings produce passive income continuously
