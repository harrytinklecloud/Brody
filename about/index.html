1. Overview Click to Expand

Brody.io is a real-time trading simulation game combined with incremental city-building mechanics. Players manage cash, net worth, stock trades, and upgrades while building their city strategically.

  • Trading dashboard & portfolio
  • Passive income from city buildings
  • Incremental upgrades & compounding earnings
  • Auto-save every 30 seconds
